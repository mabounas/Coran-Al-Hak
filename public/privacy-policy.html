Coran Al Ummah — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Coran Al Ummah ("the App") is a free Quran reading and listening application. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks ("Lecture" markers) and language/settings preferences are stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Location

Two features ask for your location: the Qibla compass and the prayer timetable. When you tap the button that starts them, the App reads your device location once and sends the coordinates to the UmmahAPI service to compute the direction of the Kaaba or the prayer times for your area. The coordinates are not stored by the App and are not linked to any identity. You can decline the permission and pick a city from a list instead, in which case no device location is read at all.

Microphone and speech recognition

The search fields offer dictation. The microphone is only opened while you hold a dictation session, started by tapping the microphone button. The recording is passed to the speech recognition service of your device or browser, which returns the recognised text; on Apple devices this may be processed by Apple's speech recognition. The App keeps no audio recording and sends none to us. If you never tap the microphone button, the microphone is never opened.

Third-party services

To display Quran text, hadiths, duas, prayer times and metadata, the App fetches data from the public UmmahAPI service (ummahapi.com). Verse commentary comes from api.quran-tafseer.com, requested through our own server. Recitation audio streams from quranicaudio.com and everyayah.com. These requests include only the technical information necessary to serve the content (for example a Surah number, or coordinates for the prayer times) — no account, name or identifier is sent. Please refer to those services' own policies for how they handle network requests.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, as no information is collected at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
