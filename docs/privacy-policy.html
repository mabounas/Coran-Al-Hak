Coran Al Hak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Coran Al Hak ("the App") is a free Quran reading and listening application. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks ("Lecture" markers) and language/settings preferences are stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Third-party services

To display Quran text and metadata, the App fetches data from the public UmmahAPI service (ummahapi.com). To play recitation audio, the App streams from quranicaudio.com. These requests include only the technical information necessary to retrieve the requested Surah (e.g. Surah number) — no personal data is sent. Please refer to those services' own policies for how they handle network requests.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, as no information is collected at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
